Morning Activation
Circuit

Eight moves, one loop. Mobility → cardio → push → squat → hinge → anti-rotation → anti-extension. Built to wake the whole system up without taxing it — this isn't your training session, it's what makes the training session (and the rest of your day) go better.

Cold joints going straight into fast movement is where niggles start — so it opens with cat-cow to unlock a stiff-from-sleeping spine, then high knees and mountain climbers to actually raise your heart rate and prime the nervous system.

The middle covers the three patterns a body needs daily: a push (press-ups), a squat, and a hip hinge (glute bridge) — most bodyweight routines skip the hinge entirely, which quietly under-trains the glutes and hamstrings that should be doing the work your lower back gets stuck compensating for.

Lower-back conscious: bird-dog and glute bridge are in here specifically for that — bird-dog builds anti-rotation control and glute activation without any spinal loading, and the bridge strengthens the posterior chain that protects the back during everything else you do. Plank finishes it off with static anti-extension core, the one thing your original list already had right.
